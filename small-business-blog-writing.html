Content care · monthly writing

Small business blog writing with a manageable monthly scope.

Answer a question your customers actually ask. Content care gives your business one useful article each month, with clear limits and drafts you can review before they go onto your website.

What is included?

One article a month, up to 1,200 words, with a title, meta description and suggested internal links, two Google Business Profile post drafts, one revision round and a monthly progress note. The quote sets the scope for one website.

Who this is for

You want to explain your services through useful articles, but do not need a large content programme. One agreed customer question each month creates a realistic starting point for a small service business.

Your agreed deliverables

  • One article a month, up to 1,200 words
  • Title, meta description and suggested internal links
  • Two Google Business Profile post drafts
  • One revision round
  • A monthly progress note

A written quote before work starts. We agree the topic, schedule, approval process and any publishing access for one website. Technical repairs, rebuilds, extra pages, directory corrections, ads and account setup are quoted separately. Rankings, traffic and AI citations cannot be guaranteed.

How the work moves forward

  1. Choose a useful question.

    We agree one topic and the facts needed to answer it. Your service knowledge supplies the specifics; the quote records the schedule, word limit and approval process for one website.

  2. Write and connect the article.

    I prepare one article of up to 1,200 words, its title and meta description, and suggestions for relevant links on your website. Two Google Business Profile post drafts use the same agreed topic.

  3. Review, then keep moving.

    You check the details and give one consolidated revision round. The monthly progress note records the completed work and a useful next step. Publishing is separate unless it is included in an agreed scope.

A question worth answering before someone enquires

Illustrative example · fictional business · not a client result

A fictional garden-maintenance business might hear: “What information should I send when asking for a garden-maintenance quote?”

A useful article could explain which areas need attention, the kind of help wanted and the preferred frequency, then point to the business’s real service page and enquiry method. Details such as pricing, availability or disposal must be confirmed by that business.

Before you choose this option

What counts as one article?

One agreed topic, up to 1,200 words. The package includes one revision round, its title and meta description, suggested internal links and two Google Business Profile post drafts. Extra articles or substantial changes to the brief are quoted separately.

Is publishing included?

Content care delivers drafts. Website publishing, account setup and technical changes are separate. Content & publishing may suit you if you want agreed help putting approved content onto existing channels.

Is this a full SEO campaign?

It is a scoped monthly writing service. It does not include unlimited content, technical repairs, directory corrections, paid ads or guaranteed rankings, traffic or AI citations.
