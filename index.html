Is this for a business like mine?

Harmon Content is for small service businesses that want clearer website content and a manageable plan for answering customers’ questions. A one-page project is a useful place to start if you’re not ready for monthly support.

How much does it cost?

Each option is quoted after a short discussion about your website and what you need. You receive the deliverables, limits, schedule and price in writing before work starts.

Will you publish the content for me?

Page refresh and Content care deliver drafts for review. Content & publishing includes publishing only on the website and channels agreed in the quote, with the access needed to do that work. New accounts and technical fixes are separate.

What do SEO, GEO and AEO mean here?

SEO helps search engines find and understand your pages. GEO and AEO describe visibility in generative and answer-based search. The work here focuses on useful answers, accurate business details, clear page structure and appropriate metadata. No service can promise a ranking or an AI citation.

Do I need a new website?

Often the useful starting point is improving the website you have. These options cover content. If a technical repair, theme change or rebuild is needed, we discuss it and quote it separately.
